2) PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
GoalX is an AI football (soccer) coaching app for players and academy athletes.
Users upload match or training clips and receive AI analysis, scores, and coaching guidance to improve performance.
It solves the problem of limited access to personal coaching feedback by delivering fast, clip-based insights on phone.
